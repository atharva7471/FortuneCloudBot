# Fortune Cloud Technologies — Complete Guide

> Fortune Cloud Technologies (FCT) is India's leading AI-integrated IT training and placement institute, headquartered in Pune, Maharashtra, with branch centers in Pimpri, Hadapsar, and Bengaluru (BTM Layout). Established in 2015 as a subsidiary of Cravita Technologies India Pvt. Ltd., FCT offers job-oriented, project-based IT courses designed around real hiring demands — not outdated textbooks. With 5,500+ students successfully placed, 5,000+ hiring partner companies, a 94% placement success rate, and a highest package of 18 LPA, Fortune Cloud Technologies bridges the gap between college education and industry-ready careers in cloud computing, full stack development, data science, cybersecurity, DevOps, AI/ML, SAP, and digital marketing.

## Why Choose Fortune Cloud Technologies

### 1. Practical Learning (80/20 Model)
At Fortune Cloud Technologies, learning happens through doing. Students spend 80% of their training time working on real-world projects that mirror actual industry tasks. No dummy exercises, no copy-paste tutorials. By the time you complete your course, you already have a working portfolio — the kind that gets you hired.

### 2. Industry Mentors
Our expert trainers are active IT professionals — developers, cloud architects, and DevOps engineers who work in the industry every day. This means students learn the tools, workflows, and best practices that companies actually use right now — not outdated curriculum. Our mentors have placed students at top MNCs across Pune, Bangalore, Hyderabad, and Mumbai.

### 3. 100% Placement Support
Our placement assistance goes beyond just sharing job links. Our dedicated placement cell works with 5,000+ hiring companies to match the right student to the right role. We cover resume building, aptitude training, mock interviews, and soft skills — giving every tool needed to walk into an interview with confidence and walk out with an offer.

### 4. Corporate Culture Environment
Fortune Cloud Technologies centres are built to feel like real IT offices, not classrooms. From day one, students experience a professional work culture — structured working hours, team-based project delivery, and code reviews — so that joining a company after training feels like a natural next step, not a culture shock.
